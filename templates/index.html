Mathematical Model & Governing Equations

All physics equations used in this optimization — hover each card for details

Motor Torque — RPM Derating
Tm = Tm0 · fd
fd = max(0.55, 1 − 5×10⁻⁵ · max(0, ωrpm − 3000))
Models motor field-weakening above 3000 RPM. Torque linearly derated to a minimum of 55% of peak.
Wheel Torque & Tractive Force
Tw = Tm · G · η
Ft = Tw / rw
G = gear ratio, η = transmission efficiency, rw = wheel radius (m).
Rolling Resistance
Frr = Crr · m · g
Proportional to vehicle weight. Crr = tyre rolling resistance coefficient (typically 0.010–0.015).
Aerodynamic Drag
Fdrag = ½ · ρ · Cd · A · v²
ρ = 1.225 kg/m³ (air density). Grows with the square of velocity — dominant at high speed.
Grade Resistance
Fgrade = m · g · sin(θ)
θ = road grade angle in radians. Gravity component opposing uphill motion.
Longitudinal Acceleration
a(G) = (Ft − Frr − Fdrag − Fgrade) / m
Net forward force divided by vehicle mass. Positive value = vehicle accelerates.
Maximum Vehicle Speed
vmax(G) = (ωmax / G) · rw · 3.6
ωmax = 8000 · 2π / 60 rad/s
Speed at maximum motor RPM (8000 RPM). Higher G reduces top speed. Converted to km/h by ×3.6.
Gradeability
GR(G) = tan(arcsin( (Ft−Frr−Fdrag) / (m·g) )) · 100
Maximum grade (%) the vehicle can climb at constant speed at the given operating point.
Power Analysis
Pm = Tm · ω   [W]
Pw = Ft · v   [W]
Ploss = Pm − Pw   [W]
Motor shaft power vs wheel power. Difference is gearbox thermal loss.
Energy Consumption
E = Pm / (vm/s · 3.6)  [Wh/km]
Instantaneous energy cost per kilometre at the given speed and gear ratio.
Composite Performance Score
S(G) = 0.4·S̃a + 0.4·S̃v + 0.2·S̃GR
S̃x = (x − xmin) / (xmax − xmin)
Weighted min-max normalized objective. 40% acceleration + 40% max speed + 20% gradeability.
Newton-Raphson Update Rule
Gn+1 = Gn − S′(Gn) / S″(Gn)
S′ ≈ [S(G+h) − S(G−h)] / 2h
S″ ≈ [S(G+h) − 2S(G) + S(G−h)] / h²
Central-difference numerical differentiation, h = 10⁻⁴. Maximizes S(G) by finding where S′(G) = 0.
